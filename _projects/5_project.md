Research assistant at the **ETH Computational Robotics Lab** (Feb 2024 -- Feb 2025).

- Implemented unsupervised skill discovery methods with discrete latent variables (DIAYN, DISDAIN) and continuous latent variables (ASE, METRA) to encode skills into a latent space for control
- Trained a policy that allows controlling height, gait patterns, pitch angle, and feet width in quadruped locomotion
- Technologies: IsaacLab, Python, ROS2, Reinforcement Learning

### Multi-Gait Quadruped Locomotion for Interactive Media Art

- Developed an RL-based locomotion controller capable of producing expressive gait patterns
- Successfully deployed the controller for a live interactive media art exhibition (AATB artistic studio)
